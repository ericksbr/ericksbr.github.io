# My Background
I am a conservation social scientist and postdoctoral fellow based at Oregon State University. I'm supervised by [Dr. Megan Jones](https://www.megansjones.com/) in the Human Dimensions lab in the Department of Fisheries, Wildlife, and Conservation Sciences. As an interdisciplinary social scientist, I listen to understand how others see and make sense of the world. I love applying my social science toolkit to address real-world conservation and environmental challenges.
